DwnLodr · Android

Privacy Policy

What DwnLodr keeps on your phone, what it sends when you use online features, and the choices you have.

Effective: 23 October 2026Last updated: 23 October 2026

At a glance

  • No DwnLodr account is required. Browsing records, settings, download records and private media are stored on your device.
  • Online features contact other services. Websites, media hosts and Google Search receive the requests needed to provide the content you choose.
  • No built-in advertising or remote analytics SDK. Websites opened inside the app may have their own ads, cookies and tracking.
  • You control local data. Clear browsing records, remove files, close private sessions, or clear app storage. Unhide private media first if you want to keep it.

01About this policy

UTK Apps ("we", "us" or "our") develops DwnLodr (the "app"), an Android application for browsing supported websites, saving permitted media, and playing and organizing media on your device. Its package name is com.dwnlodr.app.

This policy describes DwnLodr's data handling, including the features available in version 0.9.44. It covers the app and this policy webpage. Websites and services you access through the app have their own privacy policies.

DwnLodr does not operate an app account system or send your browsing history, downloads or private media to a UTK Apps backend. If you choose to email us, we receive the information you include in that email as described below.

03Network requests and web searches

When you open a website, submit a link for lookup, load a thumbnail, stream media or download content, DwnLodr contacts the relevant website, media host, content delivery network or other resources used by that page. These recipients receive the requested address and normal connection information, such as your IP address, browser or app request headers, and applicable website cookies or session information. URLs may themselves contain search terms, content identifiers or access tokens.

When you submit search words using Search on Home or Go in the browser, the app opens Google Search and sends those terms to Google over HTTPS. Google receives the search request, IP address, browser information and applicable cookies. DwnLodr does not send search words to a suggestion provider while you type. Google's processing is explained in its Privacy Policy.

Media requests go from your device to the relevant services; UTK Apps does not run a download proxy for this app. A website may redirect a request or load resources from other companies. Those recipients may process data outside your country under their own policies. Android and your network provider also handle the connectivity needed for these requests.

Copying a link places it on the device clipboard. Choosing Share opens Android's share sheet and passes the chosen link or media to the receiving app you select, whose own privacy practices then apply.

04Clipboard link checks

When the app opens or returns to the foreground with its main window focused, it checks the current clipboard once for a public web link. A suitable link is offered in a confirmation before it is pasted into Home or used for an online lookup. Declining or dismissing the offer does not open that link or start a download.

The feature ignores non-web text, private or local addresses and clips Android marks as sensitive. It does not continuously monitor the clipboard in the background. The offered link and copy timestamp are held in memory during the activity session. When you dismiss an offer, the app stores a SHA-256 hash of the address and a 24-hour expiry locally to avoid repeated prompts; it does not save the raw clipboard text for that cooldown. Expired cooldown entries are pruned on later dismissals.

Once you accept a link, the normal lookup, website-session and browsing-record behavior described in this policy applies. Android may show a clipboard-access notification.

05Website cookies and sign-ins

The regular in-app browser uses Android WebView. Websites can set cookies, including third-party cookies, and use website storage and caches. A website's sign-in session can persist between app launches. Sign-in forms belong to the website you visit; DwnLodr does not create a UTK Apps account or send website passwords to UTK Apps.

To retrieve media you have chosen, the app may use the relevant website's session cookies and request headers for page and media requests. Some lookups create temporary app-private cookie or page files that are removed when the lookup finishes. Regular-session cookies and website storage can persist separately from History and tabs.

Signing out through a website ends that service's session according to its controls. Clearing DwnLodr's storage in Android removes its locally stored website sessions. Clearing History alone does not clear cookies, sign out of websites or close open tabs.

06Incognito browsing and ad blocking

On supported WebView versions, Incognito uses a separate temporary browser profile. Its pages are not added to regular History, its tabs are not saved for restoration, and its cookies and website storage are separate from the regular browser. Closing Incognito clears that profile's browsing data and discards its temporary media snapshots. Abandoned private profiles are cleaned up on a later startup when supported by WebView.

Incognito limits local browsing records. Websites, search providers and network operators can still receive and observe the connections needed to access online content. Files you explicitly download, and their download records, can remain in the ordinary library after an Incognito session closes.

The optional Block Ads setting applies local filtering rules and page adjustments. It does not upload browsing activity to a filtering service or download a remote filter subscription. Some website ads and tracking can still load. DwnLodr itself includes no advertising, advertising-ID, remote analytics or automatic crash-reporting SDK in the version covered by this policy.

07Local WhatsApp Status access

If you use the WhatsApp Status feature, Android asks you to select the local .Statuses folder. The app retains read access to that selected folder to display locally available photos and videos. While the gallery is visible, it checks that folder for changes. It copies only statuses you select to your chosen download location and does not modify or delete the source status files.

This feature does not sign in to WhatsApp, retrieve statuses from WhatsApp's servers, or read your contacts, chat messages or account credentials. Status media is processed locally and is not sent to UTK Apps or Google Search by this feature. Content you deliberately share through another app is handled by that receiving app.

08Anonymous private media and PIN

The optional Anonymous feature moves selected photos and videos into encrypted app-private storage outside the ordinary gallery. It uses Google Tink streaming authenticated encryption with keys protected by Android Keystore. The app verifies the encrypted copy before removing the public copy.

Your 6–10 digit PIN is checked locally. A salted PIN verifier is stored instead of the PIN itself. The correct PIN entered on Home opens Anonymous and is not sent to Google. However, explicitly pressing Search with a nonmatching number submits that number as an ordinary Google search. The app does not automatically search numeric input while you type.

Private previews are processed locally; temporary preview files are cleared when previews close or the vault locks, and stale previews are cleaned on startup. Leaving Anonymous or backgrounding the app locks the private session. The vault has no developer-run PIN recovery or cloud restore service.

Keep files you need: unhide private media before clearing app storage or uninstalling DwnLodr. Clearing or uninstalling removes the vault and its keys, and UTK Apps cannot recover those files or a forgotten PIN.

09Android permissions and access

  • Internet and network state: browse websites, search, look up formats, download media and check or retrieve download-engine updates.
  • Notifications: show download and playback status. You can control notification permission in Android. Titles or status details may be visible in device notifications according to your system settings.
  • Foreground services and wake lock: keep user-started downloads, media processing and playback running as needed, including when the app is in the background.
  • Audio settings: support playback and audio controls.
  • Selected file and folder access: read or manage media you select, save downloads to a chosen folder and read the selected WhatsApp Status folder. Android's pickers provide this access without a broad all-files permission.

The app version covered by this policy does not request contacts, SMS, call logs, camera, microphone, device-location, accessibility-service or notification-listener access. Its notification permission displays the app's own notifications; it does not grant access to messages in other apps.

10Third-party libraries, services and sharing

DwnLodr uses Android and AndroidX components, Kotlin libraries, OkHttp, Coil, Media3, Google Tink, yt-dlp, youtubedl-android and FFmpeg for local application functionality and the content requests described above. These components are not a UTK Apps analytics platform.

The parties that may receive information when you use online features are:

  • Google: search requests and related browser data when you use Google Search, or information sent by a Google webpage you visit. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Websites, media platforms, embedded providers and CDNs: page, media, image and session requests needed for the content you open, look up or save. Their own policies cover their collection, cookies, advertising and retention.
  • GitHub and its delivery infrastructure: connection information when you choose to check or download a download-engine update, open GitHub links, or visit this GitHub Pages policy. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.
  • Apps or providers you select: information you deliberately share, or files accessed through a file or folder provider you choose. A cloud-backed document provider may process files under its own policy.
  • Our email provider: your address, message and any attachments if you contact us by email, so we can receive and respond to your inquiry.

We do not sell or rent personal data, use your local media or browsing records for our own advertising, or receive those records through an app telemetry service. We may disclose correspondence we actually hold if required by applicable law. This policy webpage contains no analytics script, advertising, tracking pixel or visitor form; its hosting provider still processes the connection needed to serve it.

11Retention and deletion

Data remains for the time needed by the local feature or until you remove it. The following controls explain the differences between app records, website sessions and actual files:

  • History and bookmarks: delete individual entries or clear History from the browser menu. History also drops older entries when its 200-address limit is reached. Bookmarks remain until removed.
  • Regular tabs: closing a tab removes it from the saved tab list. Closing tabs does not delete History, bookmarks or website cookies.
  • Downloads and imported media: records remain until removed or app storage is cleared. Use the app's available file-delete action or a file manager to delete actual saved files. Removing a Player reference does not delete the original file. Files in shared or user-selected storage may remain after uninstalling.
  • Cookies, site storage, settings and cached media information: retention varies by the website, cache and app feature. Some temporary lookup files are cleaned after use; older unused format snapshots are pruned. Android's Clear storage control removes the app's locally stored data and permissions associated with those records.
  • Incognito: private browsing data is cleared when the session closes, with abandoned-profile cleanup on later startup. Downloads you chose to save remain separately.
  • Clipboard dismissals: a hashed link is suppressed for 24 hours. Expired entries are pruned on subsequent dismissals; clearing app storage removes the stored hashes.
  • Anonymous vault: encrypted files remain until unhidden or app data is deleted. Unhide first to preserve them. Clearing storage or uninstalling permanently removes private files and the local keys needed to read them.
  • Privacy and support emails: we retain correspondence as needed to answer and resolve your request, then delete it unless retention is required by law. You may ask us to delete correspondence using the contact below.

To remove all app-private data, use Android Settings → Apps → DwnLodr → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app. Labels vary by device. First save or unhide files you want to keep; then separately remove any unwanted files from shared folders. DwnLodr has no developer account to delete. To delete information held by a website or other service, use that service's own privacy and account controls.

12How data is protected

App-private records use Android's application storage protections. Anonymous media has the additional encryption described above; regular download files and browsing records do not have the vault's additional encryption. Shared downloads may be available to other apps that have appropriate access.

Google searches and this policy page use HTTPS. Online content uses HTTPS when supplied by the website or media source. DwnLodr can also open HTTP content, so it would be inaccurate to promise that every possible browser or media connection is encrypted. Use trusted websites and secure connections.

The app disables Android automatic backup in its configuration and keeps the vault in non-backup storage. It does not provide a UTK Apps cloud backup service. Device or document-provider backup behavior may depend on your device and chosen provider. No device, storage system or network can be guaranteed completely secure.

13Children's privacy

DwnLodr is a general browsing and media utility and is not directed at children. Websites accessible through the browser may contain age-restricted material and are subject to their own age rules. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through a UTK Apps account or analytics service. If you believe a child has sent personal information to our contact address, email us so we can address and delete it as appropriate.

14Your privacy choices and contact

You can use the local deletion controls above, manage Android permissions, sign out of websites, choose whether to use online features, and contact us with privacy questions or requests. Depending on your location, applicable law may give you rights to access, correct or delete personal information, restrict or object to processing, receive a copy, withdraw consent where applicable, or complain to a data-protection authority.

UTK Apps cannot remotely inspect or erase information held only on your device. We can help you locate the relevant controls and handle requests concerning correspondence we receive. Website providers handle requests for the information they hold.
